Aroma Massage

Aromatherapy Massage in BTM Layout, Bengaluru

Soothe the senses with tailored essential oil blends for relaxation and stress relief.

Overview

Our Aroma Massage at Neethus Wellness Spa in BTM Layout blends expert massage techniques with aromatherapy to deliver a deeply restorative experience. We use high-quality, therapeutic-grade essential oils chosen for your needs — calming lavender for sleep support, uplifting citrus for low mood, grounding frankincense for stress relief, or bespoke blends to target tension and fatigue.

Technique

The treatment combines long flowing Swedish-style strokes with light kneading and focused pressure on areas of tension. Oil is warmed and applied in a relaxing sequence to the back, neck, shoulders, arms and legs. The therapist will adapt pressure and pace to suit your comfort and desired outcome.

Key Benefits

  • Rapid stress reduction and improved mood
  • Improved circulation and lymphatic drainage
  • Reduced muscle tension and improved relaxation
  • Enhanced sleep quality and mental clarity
  • Nourished, glowing skin from natural oil blends

    Who Is It For?

    Ideal for clients seeking gentle, restorative care — those with stress, mild muscle tension, sleep difficulties, or anyone who wants a soothing, aromatic spa experience in Bengaluru.

    Preparation & Aftercare

    Please arrive 10 minutes early to relax. Avoid heavy meals before the treatment. After your massage, drink plenty of water and allow at least 30 minutes of quiet rest to prolong the benefits.

    Duration & Booking

    Available as 60-minute sessions. For extended sessions, group or couple bookings and current pricing at our BTM Layout spa, please message us on WhatsApp to confirm availability.
