Swedish Massage

Relaxing Swedish Massage in BTM Layout, Bengaluru

Gentle, flowing strokes that ease stress and restore a feeling of lightness.

Overview

Swedish massage uses long, flowing strokes, kneading, and gentle percussion to relax the body and release surface muscle tension. Our therapists focus on creating a calming rhythm that promotes circulation, reduces stress hormones, and supports mental relaxation — an excellent choice for first-time spa visitors and those seeking restorative care in BTM Layout.

Technique

The treatment uses light to medium pressure with effleurage, petrissage, and light tapotement strokes over major muscle groups. Pressure is adjusted to client comfort and therapeutic goals.

Benefits

  • Stress reduction and relaxation
  • Improved circulation and lymphatic flow
  • Reduced muscle soreness and stiffness
  • Enhanced sense of wellbeing and restful sleep
