Hot Stone Massage

Hot Stone Massage in BTM Layout, Bengaluru

Warm volcanic stones combined with expert massage to melt away tension and restore calm.

Overview

Hot Stone therapy uses smooth, warmed basalt stones placed along energy points and used as massage tools to transfer heat deeply into muscle tissue. The therapeutic warmth eases tight muscles, increases circulation and provides a gentle, calming experience — perfect for clients seeking restorative warmth and deep relaxation in BTM Layout.

Technique

Stones are heated to a comfortable temperature and placed on the back, palms, and along the legs to warm muscles. Therapists also use stones as extensions of the hands to perform long, gliding strokes, allowing heat to penetrate deeper tissue layers while applying soothing pressure.

Benefits & Aftercare

  • Deep muscle relaxation and reduced stiffness
  • Improved circulation and toxin removal
  • Enhanced sense of warmth and comfort
  • Aftercare: hydrate well and avoid cold exposure immediately after treatment
