Four Hand Massage — Double the Relaxation in BTM Layout

A synchronised twin-therapist massage for an immersive, deeply relaxing experience.

Overview

Four Hand Massage doubles the therapist attention: two trained therapists work in synchrony to deliver simultaneous strokes, creating an immersive rhythm that quickly dissolves tension. This luxurious treatment enhances relaxation, balances sensory input, and leaves clients with a profound sense of calm — perfect for special occasions or when you want an elevated spa experience in BTM Layout.

Technique & Experience

Therapists coordinate mirror-image movements across your back, shoulders and limbs, using gentle to medium pressure as requested. The synchronized rhythm produces a unique sensory effect that deepens relaxation beyond a single-therapist session.

Benefits

  • Enhanced relaxation through synchronized touch
  • Deep release of surface tension and improved circulation
  • Heightened sensory relaxation and stress relief
  • Ideal for couples or solo pampering sessions

Booking & Preparation

Available as 60-minute sessions. Bookings are recommended in advance due to the double-therapist requirement. Please use the WhatsApp button to check therapist availability and to book a preferred time at our BTM Layout spa.
