Thai Massage

Thai Massage & Assisted Stretching in BTM Layout

A dynamic therapy combining assisted stretching and pressure work to increase mobility and release tension.

Overview

Thai massage is a traditional system of bodywork combining rhythmic compression, assisted stretching and acupressure along energy lines (sen) to restore mobility and ease tension. Our trained therapists guide you through gentle stretches and targeted pressure to improve flexibility, circulation and overall movement — perfect for clients in BTM Layout seeking functional recovery and lasting relief.

Technique

Performed on a comfortable mat, the therapist will use hands, palms, thumbs and forearms to apply rhythmic compressions and holds, combined with passive stretching of shoulders, hips and legs. Techniques are adjusted to suit your flexibility and comfort level.

Benefits

  • Improved joint mobility and flexibility
  • Reduced muscle stiffness and chronic tension
  • Better posture and movement patterns
  • Increased circulation and energy flow
  • Suitable for athletic warm-ups and recovery

Who It Suits

Ideal for active clients, those with restricted movement, or anyone seeking a dynamic therapeutic session. Not recommended during late pregnancy or immediately after major surgery.
