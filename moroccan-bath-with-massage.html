Moroccan Bath Ritual in BTM Layout, Bengaluru

A rich, detoxifying hammam combined with a rejuvenating massage for radiant skin and total relaxation.

Overview

Our Moroccan Bath (hammam) ritual is a traditional cleansing ceremony that includes warmth, exfoliation and a restorative massage. The treatment starts with gentle steaming, followed by a full-body scrub using natural clay or black soap to remove dead skin, and finishes with a nourishing oil massage to hydrate and soften the skin — leaving you radiant and refreshed in BTM Layout.

What to Expect

  • Warm steam to open pores
  • Exfoliating scrub to remove dead skin and impurities
  • Relaxing body massage and moisturising finish

    Benefits & Aftercare

    Experience smoother, softer skin, improved circulation and a deep sense of relaxation. Aftercare includes avoiding heavy sun exposure for 24 hours and using a hydrating lotion to maintain softness.
