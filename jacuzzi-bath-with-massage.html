Jacuzzi Bath with Massage

Hydrotherapy & Massage in BTM Layout, Bengaluru

Soothing hydrotherapy massage combining warm jets and expert bodywork to relax muscles and boost circulation.

Overview

Our Jacuzzi Bath combines targeted warm-jet hydrotherapy with restorative massage to ease muscle tension, reduce swelling and stimulate circulation. The alternating pressure of jets and hands supports recovery after exercise and helps melt away daily stress — a luxurious treatment available at our BTM Layout spa.

What to Expect

After a brief consultation, you will recline in a comfortably heated jacuzzi where warm jets will be applied to key muscle groups. A therapist will then follow with focused massage work to optimise relaxation and recovery.

Benefits & Aftercare

  • Reduced muscle soreness and improved circulation
  • Relief from fatigue and boosted recovery
  • Aftercare: hydrate, avoid heavy exertion immediately after treatment

Booking

Available as 60-minute sessions. Please inform us of any skin conditions or cardiovascular concerns when booking. Reserve your jacuzzi session via WhatsApp for preferred slots at our BTM Layout centre.
